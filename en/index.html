Česká verze

In short

  • Your data belongs to your Apple ID. The app stores it on your device and syncs it between your devices through iCloud (CloudKit).
  • The developer receives no data from you and has no access to your data in iCloud. The app has no own servers, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • You share data with other people only through an iCloud invitation. Only people you invite and who accept can see it.
  • The app uses only Apple services: Sign in with Apple, iCloud (CloudKit) and the Apple Push Notification service (APNs).

Sync through iCloud

  • Data is stored in your private iCloud database (CloudKit) under your Apple ID, so it is the same on all your devices.
  • Apple stores and protects data in iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.
  • The app uses the iCloud user identifier only to give each person the same ID on all devices (yours and your partner's). It is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Sync requires being signed in to iCloud on the device. When the iCloud account on the device is signed out or switched, the app removes the previous account's data from the device (it stays in iCloud) and signs you out.

Sharing with your partner and household

You can share only through an iCloud invitation (Apple's system feature). Only the invitation decides who has access – you see those people in the app under "Shared with" for a calendar or list.

  • Shared calendar: its events including their moods. Private events appear only as "Busy" with their time, never their title or notes.
  • Shared shopping list: the list and its items.
  • Household: chores, goals, challenges, date ideas, moods and replies to them, tasks and journal entries you share with someone, and alarms you propose to your partner.
  • People in a share see your name from iCloud. They can read the shared data and, depending on the permission chosen in the invitation, edit it.
  • The app asks for your consent before you share for the first time.
  • You can stop sharing a calendar or list at any time; your partner then loses access to it.

You can also send data yourself through the system Share menu (Messages, Mail, AirDrop…): an event or calendar as an .ics file (without moods), a task as .ics or text, a journal entry or shopping list as text, and the full export. What happens to it then is up to the recipient and the app they use.

The app does not sell data, does not share it with advertising platforms or other third parties, and does not use device identifiers to recognize you.

Sign in with Apple

Apple handles the sign-in. The app receives the account identifier and, on the first sign-in, your name and e-mail if you share them (you can hide your e-mail). The name and e-mail are stored in your profile.

Face ID and Touch ID

The journal, private events and deleting all data can be protected with Face ID or Touch ID. Biometric data is processed only by the system on your device. The app never sees, stores or transmits it; it receives only the result (success or failure).

Notifications

  • Local notifications created on your device: event reminders, mood prompts and challenge check-in reminders, if you turn them on.
  • Notifications about your partner's changes: when your partner adds or changes something in a share, your device creates them after syncing. Choose the kinds and quiet hours in Settings › Notifications.
  • iCloud sends silent pushes to your devices through the Apple Push Notification service (APNs) so they know there is something to sync. They don't contain your data.
  • Notifications are never used for advertising. Their content may appear on the lock screen depending on your system preview settings.
  • Alarms are scheduled by the system on your device. Widgets and the date countdown read data stored on the device.

How to turn them off: in the app's Settings, or any time in the system Settings › Notifications › CoupleTender.

Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your devices and in your iCloud until you delete it.
  • One by one you can delete events, calendars (except the last one), tasks, journal entries, shopping lists, items and people. Removing a person also deletes their moods, replies, chore completions and check-ins everywhere.
  • Everything at once in Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all data, with two options:
    • Only on this device – deletes the data, settings and notifications on the device; iCloud and your other devices keep the data.
    • Everywhere, including iCloud – also deletes your data in iCloud and on your other devices. Your partner loses access to what you shared, and you leave what others shared with you (their data stays).
  • Signing out of the app keeps the data. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device, not in iCloud.
  • Temporary files created for sharing are deleted the next time the app starts.

Export

In Settings › Privacy & data › Export you create one ZIP file: calendars.ics (all events, opens in any calendar app) and backup.json (complete backup of events, tasks, journal, shopping lists, goals, chores, challenges, date ideas, people and calendars). Moods are not exported.

Children

CoupleTender is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. You can add children as household members (for example for chores); you enter their details and the child doesn't use the app.

Changes to this policy

We will announce changes that expand the collection or sharing of data in the app in advance. The current version is always in the app (Settings › Privacy & data) and on this page.
