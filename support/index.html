Frequently asked questions

How do I share a calendar, a shopping list or the household with my partner?

Open Settings › Household › Invite via iCloud, or edit a calendar or shopping list and choose Invite via iCloud. Your partner accepts the iCloud invitation and then appears under "Shared with". You can stop sharing at any time.

My data does not appear on another device.

  • Check that you are signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud is turned on for CoupleTender (System Settings › Apple ID › iCloud).
  • Open Settings › About in the app to see the sync status and tap "Sync again".
  • Make sure the device is online. Sync can take a short while.

My partner does not see my changes.

Changes are visible only in what you share: shared calendars, shopping lists and the household. Check that the invitation was accepted (Settings › Household, or the calendar's "Shared with" list).

How do I colour an event?

Open the event and use the Colour section, or tap + in the calendar and choose Colour events to colour several at once. Events with the same name in the same calendar share the colour.

Notifications or widgets do not work.

Check that notifications are allowed in System Settings › Notifications › CoupleTender, and set the kinds and quiet hours in the app under Settings › Notifications. Alarms need iOS 26.1 or later.

How do I export or delete my data?

Settings › Privacy & data lets you export a ZIP (calendar as .ics plus a full backup) or delete all data, either only on this device or everywhere including iCloud.
